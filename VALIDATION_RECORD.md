## Statement

This record documents that NavalForge has demonstrated an integrated computational workflow for weights and centers, hydrostatic equilibrium, intact stability, resistance, and planing-performance assessment for a controlled small-craft example.

The public case was intentionally created with synthetic particulars and a generic vessel representation. It does not reproduce a customer vessel, production design, native CAD model, or confidential engineering dataset.

## Verification basis

The demonstration results were reviewed for internal consistency and compared with an independent naval-design reference workflow, including Orca3D-based checks. The published claim is limited to agreement within the controlled case study and does not imply universal equivalence for every hull form or operating condition.

## Evidence included

- Six-page English validation carousel
- Public product interface demonstration
- High-level calculation and comparison methodology
- Public-data governance and automated content checks

## Approval boundary

This public record is evidence of software-development and verification progress. It is not a class certificate, flag-state approval, regulatory endorsement, or final engineering approval for a real vessel.
